Reflection on typebot and metabase

May 10, 2026

When the instructor said that unit four is about the projects on communicating information and in this we have to work on two open ended projects in which we have to make a chatbot using typebot and a dashboard using metabase. As the resources for this are not provided we have to do our research and understand the concepts.. Working on the two projects chatbot and metabase was really interesting and it was a new experience as we are doing the entire process from understanding to implementing the concepts.. In the beginning it was a bit confusing and challenging as the platform was entirely new for me and there are lots of different resources available in the internet and I don’t know where to start but later I visited the official websites, explored the things and watched some videos regarding this on youtube. After this I got an idea of what to do and how to do it. The demonstration given by the instructor was also really helpful..The making of the chatbot flow was similar to the making of the prototype in the user experience designing process in which also we have to connect the screens, buttons.working on these projects was a great experience where I gained the experience of how to work on an open ended project in limited time. I would like to share that I felt very happy when my friends saw the chatbot and they said that it is very nice. They explored my chatbot very eagerly and this boosted my confidence.overall this was a very useful and practical learning experience.
