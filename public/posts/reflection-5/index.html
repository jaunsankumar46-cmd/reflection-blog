Reflection on experience of women in computer science-group 4

May 10, 2026

The presentation I would like to discuss is the presentation of group 4 and their topic is the experience of women in computer science. Their presentation was really good, every one presented the slides and their ideas very clearly, every one was very confident and the information provided was new to me. The presentation discussed the issue in a clear and interesting way.the group explained that choosing computerscience among women depends on various factors like family support, peer influence etc,..my thoughts also align with what the group presented i also believe that support from the family, friends and teachers play a major role for women to choose computer science . in their presentation i noticed an interesting thing that is the participation of women in the computer science field in India is increasing while the participation of women in united states in computer science field is decreasing.from their presentation i knew that in earlier time women participation was also high but after some time due to the male dominance the participation of women in computer science field is gradually and in the recent times the participation of women is again increasing. Normally the people saw the field of computer science as a safe career option.The thing that the group may include in the presentation is may be a motivating real life example which will make the presentation more relatable. In some of the slides there are too many points which I think they can reduce and can use images.Overall the presentation was good, informative and engaging .
