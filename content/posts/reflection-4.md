When the instructor said that unit four is about the projects on communicating information and in this we have to work on two open ended projects in which we have to make a chatbot using typebot and a dashboard using metabase. As  the resources for this are not provided we have to do our research and  understand the concepts.. Working on the two projects chatbot and metabase was really interesting and it was a new experience as we are doing the entire process from understanding to implementing the concepts..
