When the topic was first introduced by the instructor. it was not something entirely new for me .I just know that failure is defined as "not being capable of doing something " , and it just means we have failed to achieve our goal. But later I understand that failure is not something to  worry about but it is something that we have to be curious about , why this failure happened , and what should I do such that it won't occur in the future , is there any useful information available in this failure that can be useful. I learnt that there are different types of failure and Intelligent failure is a failure that happens in a new territory , while pursuing a desired goal, done after a proper research or homework, and the failure is very small such that it is also useful. I read many examples in the readings where the experiment or the research is not stopped after a failure, they just take a lesson from the failure and continued to work on the experiment or research. I liked the quote by Thomas Alva Edison " I have not failed I have just found thousands way that won't work". the Instructor also give some reasons why the failure happens She said that failure may happen due to the lack of communication, I also faced this sometimes in our group assignment or presentation there are some confusion due to the lack of communication among our group members. The main takeaway from this unit is failure is just a step of success , failure is a part of life and we should not worry about it if it is an intelligent failure.
